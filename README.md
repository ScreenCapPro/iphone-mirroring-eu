# iPhone Mirroring on Mac in the EU

**Phone Mirror** is a native macOS app that mirrors your iPhone or iPad screen to your Mac, wirelessly over AirPlay or wired over USB. It works in the European Union, where Apple's built-in iPhone Mirroring feature is not available.

No jailbreak. No VPN or region change. Any Apple Account.

## Why this exists

Apple's iPhone Mirroring (iOS 18 / macOS Sequoia) isn't available in the EU. Searching for a fix usually leads to scripts that change system settings, require an American Apple ID, or stop working after an update.

Phone Mirror takes a different route: it connects to your device directly through **AirPlay** or **USB/Lightning**, so it works the same way in any country. It's a regular Mac app you download and open.

## Features

**Connection**
- Wireless mirroring over AirPlay on your local network, with automatic device discovery
- Wired mirroring over USB or Lightning for the lowest latency
- iPhone and iPad supported (Apple TV coming soon)
- Works with devices signed into a different iCloud account, so you can mirror a friend's or colleague's device

**Capture**
- Screen recording with device bezels and custom backdrop colors
- Full-resolution screenshots
- Audio mirroring, so you hear your device on your Mac in real time

**Window and display**
- Full screen mode for demos and presentations
- Always-on-top window
- Auto-rotate when your device rotates
- Auto-matching bezels and colors for your actual device
- Pixel-perfect sizing, either true-to-life or exact pixels
- Sleep and lock detection

**Setup**
- Guided setup wizard for connecting new devices

## Requirements

- Mac running **macOS 13 Ventura or later**
- iPhone or iPad
- For wireless mirroring: both devices on the same local network
- For wired mirroring: a USB or Lightning cable

iPhone XR/Duo is not currently supported.

## Install

1. [Download Phone Mirror](https://phonemirror.app/#download) (`.dmg`).
2. Open the disk image and drag **Phone Mirror** to **Applications**.
3. Launch the app and follow the setup wizard.

## FAQ

**Does it work in the EU?**
Yes. Phone Mirror works anywhere, including all EU countries. It connects to your device directly over AirPlay or USB, so it doesn't depend on Apple's regional availability of iPhone Mirroring.

**Do I need an American Apple ID, a VPN, or a jailbreak?**
No. No region change, no VPN, and no jailbreak. Any Apple Account works.

**What's the difference from Apple's iPhone Mirroring?**
Apple's feature lets you control your iPhone from the Mac. Phone Mirror shows your iPhone or iPad screen on your Mac and focuses on viewing, recording, screenshots and presenting.

**Is it a subscription?**
No. One-time purchase with lifetime updates included.

**Is there a free version?**
Yes. Mirroring over AirPlay and USB/Lightning is included in the free version. Lifetime removes the watermark and adds recording, high-res screenshots and audio.

**Which Macs and iPhones are supported?**
Any Mac on macOS 13 Ventura or later. iPhone and iPad are supported; iPhone XR/Duo currently isn't.

**It's not connecting. What now?**
See the [troubleshooting guide](https://phonemirror.app/troubleshooting). If that doesn't solve it, open an issue (see below).
